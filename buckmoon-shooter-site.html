Two ways to deploy

Pick your engagement

SOLO OPS

Five Waves, One Checkpoint

Hostile drones escalate wave over wave — four in the first push, thirteen by the last. Hold until the checkpoint is secured, or go down trying. Every run tracks how far you got and how many you took with you.

HEAD-TO-HEAD

Face Another Player

Host a match and send the code to someone, or join theirs. First one standing wins. It's played over a live connection, so expect the honest feel of a scrappy online match rather than a perfectly smooth one.

Under the hood

What's actually in the loadout

Your own hands
A visible weapon rig with gloved hands gripping the foregrip and pistol grip — not just a floating gun.
A real reload
Empty the mag and the whole rig dips, the spent magazine drops, a fresh one slides home, and the bolt racks — not a menu, an animation.
Hostiles that move like it
Enemies walk with a real gait and switch to a lunging attack the moment they close the distance, instead of gliding at you.
Sound built from scratch
Every footstep, gunshot, and confirmed kill is synthesized live in your browser — nothing to download separately, nothing to fail to load.
Lit like it means it
A dusk sky, real-time shadows, and a manual bloom pass make muzzle flashes and tracers actually glow.
Before you deploy

How to run it

Controls

W A S D — move Mouse — aim
Click — fire R — reload Esc — pause

What you need

  • A modern desktop browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
  • No install — open the file and press Deploy
  • For Head-to-Head, an opponent with the same room code
Heads up: this is a single HTML file built to run entirely in your browser. Head-to-Head play depends on a live connection between both players and will feel a beat behind real time rather than instant — that's the honest trade-off of a browser-based match, not a bug.
